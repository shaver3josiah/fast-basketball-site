The Fine Print, Up Front
  • 3 month minimum. Memberships run 3 or 6 months from the day you register.
  • All sales are final. Sessions can be rescheduled with notice. Payments are not refunded.
  • 24 hours notice to reschedule. Miss a session without notice and it is forfeited, with no private makeup.
  • Auto-renews unless you cancel in writing 7 days before the end of a 3 month term, or 60 days before the end of a 6 month term.
  • Early exit costs 75% of what is left on the contract.

Read the full terms and training agreement before your enrollment call, so nothing on the call is a surprise.

Who Trains Here

Middle school through senior year.

Wherever your player starts, the target is the same: be undeniable when it counts. What changes is how far out we are building from.

11-13

Middle School Build

Every college player was once a middle schooler with a habit nobody caught. We catch it now, while it is still easy to fix: feet, balance, both hands. Nothing should be capping you at sixteen.

14-16

High School Jump

This is the level where players separate. We build the speed, the strength through contact, and the decisions that turn a rotation player into a starter coaches build around.

16-18

College Track

Time to get in front of the right staffs. Coach Blake sat on their side of the recruiting table. He knows what they need to see on your film, he will tell you the truth about the gap, and then he will train you to close it.
