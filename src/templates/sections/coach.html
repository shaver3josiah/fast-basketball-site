Blake Kingsley Founder & Head Skills Coach
Meet Your Coach

He builds the player you are trying to become.

Blake Kingsley spent the last two seasons on college coaching staffs, first with the Moberly Area Greyhounds, who went 25 and 7 and won the NJCAA Region 16 championship, then with Robert Morris, who won the first Horizon League title in program history and went to the NCAA Tournament. Fifty-one wins. Two championships. Two national tournaments.

He is not the coach who yells to look intense. He is the one who catches that your left foot is landing early, fixes it, and then tells you exactly what you did right on the very next rep. Players go hard for him because every correction is specific to them and every session is built for the player in front of him, not pulled from a script.

He has also sat on the college side of recruiting, grading high school film and deciding which players earned an offer. Every South Florida family gets that perspective from the first session: a straight read on where your player stands, and a plan to close the gap that college staffs actually respect.

Fast Basketball is Blake's program. Right now he runs every session himself, which is why enrollment is capped and why the method is written down as five steps anyone can check. As the program grows, every coach who joins will teach those same five steps and log the same numbers, and Blake will still set the standard. The families enrolling now are the first group trained to it.
