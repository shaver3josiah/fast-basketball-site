Meet Your Coach

He builds the player
you are trying
to become.

Blake Kingsley spent the last two seasons on college coaching staffs, first with the Moberly Area Greyhounds, who went 25 and 7 and won the NJCAA Region 16 championship, then with Robert Morris, who won the first Horizon League title in program history and went to the NCAA Tournament. Fifty-one wins. Two championships. Two national tournaments.

He is not the coach who yells to look intense. He is the one who catches that your left foot is landing early, fixes it, and then tells you exactly what you did right on the very next rep. Players go hard for him because they can tell he is actually watching them, not running a script.

He has also been on the other side of the recruiting table, watching film and deciding which high school players were worth a scholarship. That is the perspective he brings to every Miami family who walks into a session: a straight read on where you are, and a real plan for closing the gap.
