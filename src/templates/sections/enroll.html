How to Enroll

Four steps.

The right fit matters more than the pitch. A short call tells us about your player and their goals, and tells you whether group training is the right place to start.

Limited availability. Let’s talk!

01

Book Your Call

Send the form below or text (503) 686-8371. You get a 15 to 20 minute phone or Zoom call with Coach Blake about your player: position, playing time, confidence, and the one open day a week.

02

Evaluation Session

Sixty minutes on court. Coach Blake gets to know your player and finds out exactly where they are.

03

Enrollment Call

Same day or within 24 hours, while it is fresh. What we saw, what we would work on, and which term and frequency fit. You choose.

04

Start Training

Read the agreement, then book your call: Coach Blake confirms the term and the frequency that fit, then sends your agreement and payment link. A welcome email confirms your first session and your cancellation date.

Session day, every session

Arrive 15 minutes early to warm up. Bring water, your own basketball, basketball shoes, and your journal. Late arrival forfeits the time, so build in the buffer.
