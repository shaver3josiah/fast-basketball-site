How to Enroll

Four steps. About a week.

Coach Blake does not sell over text. Every family goes through the same four steps, so by the time you pay, you have met him, he has seen your player, and you both know it fits.

01

Book a Call

Send the form below or text (503) 686-8371. You get a 15 to 20 minute phone or Zoom call with Coach Blake about your player: position, playing time, confidence, and the one open day a week.

02

Evaluation Session

Sixty minutes on court. $50, or $35 if you book within 48 hours of the call. He gets to know your player and finds out exactly where they are.

03

Enrollment Call

Same day or within 24 hours, while it is fresh. What he saw, what he would work on, and which term and frequency fit. You choose.

04

Enroll Online

Read the agreement, pick pay in full, split, or monthly, and type your name to agree. A welcome email confirms your first session and your cancellation date.

Session day, every session

Arrive 15 minutes early to warm up. Bring water, your own basketball, basketball shoes, and your journal. Late arrival forfeits the time, so build in the buffer.
