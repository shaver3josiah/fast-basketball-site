Dribble Counter

Fast Basketball · Blake Kingsley

Phone on the floor inside the court, screen up. It counts every bounce and tells you whether you are holding your pace.

How it works

It hears the ball from about 20 ft. No audio is recorded and no sound leaves the phone. It does keep a log of the numbers it measured from each sound, so a count that comes out wrong can be fixed later instead of argued about.

The microphone only runs while this is on.

Feedback

Flood: the whole screen goes green while you hold the pace, blue when you drift slow, orange when you race. Readable from the far end of the floor.

Your ball

It counts your ball and not the gym by listening for two things at once: the thud of a floor taking the ball's weight, and the ring of the air sealed inside it. Plenty in a gym makes one of those. Almost nothing makes both. Every ball rings a little differently, and the same ball changes as it loses air, so it learns yours from five bounces rather than assuming. Changed balls, moved courts, or it is missing bounces? Teach it again.

Normal

Missing real bounces? Drag left. Counting something that is not the ball? Drag right, and if that does not fix it, send what it heard with the button below. The fix for that one is in the code, not on this slider.

Every sound it looked at, as numbers: when, how much it looked like your ball, how much floor it carried, and which rule turned it down. No audio, and nothing you said. Send it with a note about what it got wrong and the next version gets fixed against your gym instead of a recording of somebody else's.
